1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the BG Admin mobile application, BG Admin web application where applicable, administrative dashboards, backend APIs, business management systems, authentication systems, reporting systems, and related websites or services that link to this Privacy Policy.

BG Admin is an administrative and business-management platform designed for authorized personnel who manage business operations, including products, orders, customers, delivery operations, inventory, financial information, reports, and other operational data.

This Privacy Policy primarily applies to administrators, employees, managers, business owners, delivery coordinators, and other authorized personnel who are granted access to BG Admin.

This policy does not necessarily govern the privacy practices of customers or end users of separate Brand Gallery consumer-facing applications. Those users may be covered by a separate privacy policy applicable to the relevant consumer application.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how BG Admin is used, we may process several categories of information.

2.1 Administrator Account Information

When an administrator account is created or used, we may process information such as:

  • Administrator name
  • Email address
  • Username or administrator ID
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Account role
  • Permissions and access level
  • Authentication information
  • Login activity
  • Account status
  • Security and authorization records

Passwords are used for authentication purposes and should never be stored or transmitted in plain-text form. We do not intentionally store administrator passwords in readable plain-text form.

2.2 Business and Operational Data

BG Admin may process business information entered into or accessed through the administrative system, including:

  • Product information
  • Product names and descriptions
  • Product images
  • Product prices and costs
  • Inventory and stock information
  • Orders and order items
  • Order status
  • Payment-related operational information
  • Shipping information
  • Delivery information
  • Customer information
  • Customer contact information
  • Business reports
  • Revenue information
  • Expense information
  • Profit and loss information
  • Sales analytics
  • Product performance information
  • Administrative notes
  • Other information necessary to operate the business

The information available to an administrator depends on the administrator's assigned role and permissions.

2.3 Customer Information Accessible Through BG Admin

Because BG Admin is an administrative management platform, authorized personnel may be able to access customer information necessary to fulfill orders and operate the business.

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order information
  • Order history
  • Delivery status
  • Shipping information
  • Customer-provided information necessary to complete an order

Administrators are expected to access and use customer information only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable laws and internal policies.

4. Database and Infrastructure

BG Admin uses third-party infrastructure and technology providers to operate its services. Depending on the specific deployment and configuration, these technologies may include the following.

Neon

We may use Neon to host and operate PostgreSQL database infrastructure. Business and operational data may be stored using databases hosted through Neon infrastructure.

PostgreSQL

PostgreSQL may be used as the database technology supporting BG Admin. It may store administrator accounts, products, orders, customers, inventory, financial records, reports, and operational data.

Drizzle ORM

We may use Drizzle ORM as a software library that communicates between our backend application and PostgreSQL databases. Drizzle ORM is part of our application infrastructure and is not intended to function as an independent advertising or analytics service.

Cloudflare Workers

We may use Cloudflare Workers and related Cloudflare infrastructure to operate backend services, APIs, routing, security controls, and application functions.

Leaflet

BG Admin may use Leaflet as a client-side mapping library to display geographic information associated with business operations.

Mapping Services

Depending on our configuration, map tiles, geocoding, or related mapping functionality may be provided by separate third-party services.

Infrastructure and Privacy

These technologies are used as part of the technical infrastructure supporting BG Admin. The exact providers used may change as our infrastructure evolves.

5. Location Information

Location information requires special clarification. BG Admin may process location information that is already part of business operations, such as customer delivery addresses, delivery destinations, store locations, or other geographic information associated with an order.

This information may be used to fulfill orders, coordinate deliveries, display delivery destinations, support logistics, and manage business operations.

We Do Not Continuously Track Administrator Locations

BG Admin does not continuously track the physical location of administrators. We do not intentionally use the Admin App to continuously monitor, record, or track an administrator's GPS location in the background, and we do not use BG Admin to create a continuous personal location history of administrators.

The application is not designed to continuously monitor administrator GPS locations, create a permanent personal location history, track administrators while the application is closed, sell administrator location information, or use administrator location information for advertising.

If a specific operational feature requests device location permission, that permission is intended to support the stated feature and does not automatically mean that BG Admin continuously tracks the administrator.

Administrators may still be able to view or manage location information belonging to customers, delivery destinations, stores, or other operational entities when such information is necessary for business operations. This is different from tracking the administrator's own physical location.

Mapping Technologies

BG Admin may use Leaflet and compatible mapping services to display operational geographic information, including customer delivery locations, stores, delivery destinations, and business locations.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful governmental requests, protect our users or business, investigate fraud, prevent security incidents, enforce agreements, or protect rights, property, or safety.

7. Administrator Responsibilities

BG Admin is an administrative platform. Authorized administrators may have access to sensitive business and customer information.

Administrators are responsible for:

  • Keeping login credentials confidential
  • Using only their assigned account
  • Not sharing passwords
  • Preventing unauthorized access
  • Using customer information only for legitimate business purposes
  • Following applicable privacy and data protection laws
  • Logging out of shared or public devices
  • Reporting suspected unauthorized access

Administrators should never copy, export, disclose, or misuse customer information unless authorized to do so.

  • Password-based authentication
  • Access controls

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide BG Admin services, maintain business records, operate the business, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize, or securely archive it, subject to legal, operational, and backup requirements.

10. Data Deletion

Where applicable, administrators or authorized business representatives may request deletion of information associated with an administrator account.

Certain information may need to be retained for legal compliance, accounting, tax obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, business recordkeeping, or backup systems.

Deleting an administrator account does not necessarily mean that all business records accessible through that account will be deleted. Business records may need to remain available to authorized personnel for legitimate operational and legal purposes.

11. Data Accuracy

We rely on administrators and authorized personnel to provide accurate and current information.

If information stored in BG Admin is inaccurate or incomplete, authorized personnel may be able to update it through the application or may contact the appropriate system administrator.

12. Children's Privacy

BG Admin is intended for use by authorized business personnel and administrators. It is not designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for the purpose of providing administrative services.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in connection with BG Admin, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.
